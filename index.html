Why touch, why now?

While touch is fundamental for human perception, it remains underutilized in robotics compared to vision or audition. This workshop takes a vertical view—from biological inspiration and sensor design to simulation, perception, and control—to bridge foundational insights with deployable robotic policies.

Through keynote talks, industry perspectives, lightning sessions, posters, and a panel discussion, we bring together robotics and AI communities to chart a roadmap for integrating tactile sensing into in-the-wild manipulation.

Workshop scope

Bioinspiration

Functional principles of human and animal touch inspiring artificial tactile systems.

Transduction & Design

Exploring optical and magnetic sensing, design trade-offs, materials, morphology, and system integration.

Simulation

From high-fidelity FEM modeling to real-time simulators — bridging the sim-to-real gap in tactile sensing and control.

Perception & Touch Processing

Learning from tactile data — domain adaptation, and multimodal fusion with vision and proprioception.

Control for Manipulation

Enhancing multimodal control policies in imitation learning and reinforcement learning through tactile sensing.
